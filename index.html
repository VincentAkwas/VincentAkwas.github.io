About

Laboratory results shape clinical decisions, yet most people never learn to read them. I’m a fourth-year MLS student and self-taught developer working to close that gap with tools that are free, fast and built for the people who need them.

My work covers lab-result interpretation, case-based learning, psychometric assessments, research statistics and AI-driven automation. I’ve completed clinical placements at the Greater Accra Regional Hospital (Ridge) and Nyaho Medical Centre, and I write about laboratory science on my blog.

Before science I managed stock and customers at an auto-parts business, which taught me accuracy and how to stay calm under pressure.

Journey

2026 – Present

Final-year project, Immunology and Serology

A group study comparing Widal test reactivity with Typhidot IgM/IgG results among patients with suspected typhoid fever at a selected hospital in Ghana. It measures how well the two methods agree and examines discordant results alongside age, duration of fever and previous antibiotic use.

Flagship · Lab interpretation

LabLens Pro

A free medical lab result interpreter. Enter values across 18 panels (CBC, metabolic, lipid, liver, thyroid, coagulation, diabetes, electrolytes, enzymes, vitamins, tumour markers, hormones and more) and get flagged abnormals with sex-specific reference ranges for 95+ analytes. Includes 22 clinical calculators, PDF and image report upload, a practice mode, and offline install.

Educational tool. Not a substitute for professional medical advice.
