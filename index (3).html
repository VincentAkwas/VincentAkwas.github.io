About Me

Science, precision
& real-world impact.

I am a third-year Medical Laboratory Science student who does not wait to graduate before building a career. While most students sit in lecture halls waiting for the world to begin, I have already walked the wards of Greater Accra Regional Hospital (Ridge), run quality control in a functioning clinical lab, and shipped a live digital health tool used by real people — all before finishing my degree.

My background is deliberately broad. A hospital administration attachment at Nyaho Medical Centre gave me a window into healthcare operations beyond the bench. A stint managing a fast-paced automotive business taught me inventory control, customer relations, and how to stay sharp under pressure. These aren't distractions from my science — they make me a sharper, more versatile scientist.

I believe the future of healthcare in Africa belongs to people who can hold a pipette in one hand and write clean code with the other. I am building myself to be one of those people.

Live · Digital Health Tool
LabLens — MLS Lab Result Interpreter

A fully functional, browser-based Medical Laboratory Science tool that interprets clinical lab results with intelligent flagging and auto-generated patient summary reports. Built entirely from scratch with zero dependencies, deployed publicly on GitHub Pages.

  • 6 complete clinical panels: CBC, BMP/CMP, Liver, Lipid, Thyroid, Coagulation
  • Sex-specific reference ranges for all analytes
  • Intelligent flagging: Normal / Low / High / Critical with per-analyte commentary
  • Auto-generated patient summary reports
  • Zero dependencies — pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
